# Design History

## Angomarket
- **Product:** Institutional company presentation website.
- **Studio:** Business Studio.
- **Approved style:** Editorial Commerce.
- **Concept:** The New Commerce Journal. A commerce-focused editorial presentation built around oversized typographic statements, port and market photography, asymmetrical composition, and a strong numbered narrative.
- **Typography:** Archivo Black for display, Playfair Display for editorial emphasis, Manrope for reading, DM Mono for metadata.
- **Palette:** Angomarket golds, deep blues, sand beige, and teal, using the exact supplied brand colors.
- **Signature composition:** Full-bleed port-image hero with transparent overlay navigation; large stacked headline; oversized section numerals; alternating paper, navy, gold, and teal fields; asymmetric photo mosaic; horizontal typographic ticker.
- **Motion:** Restrained scroll reveals, continuous editorial ticker, image hover scale, responsive mobile navigation; reduced-motion preference supported.
- **Avoided patterns:** Red/black portfolio art direction, beige/olive real-estate palette, generic centered corporate hero, repeated three-card feature grids, fabricated company claims and testimonials.
- **Content status:** Provisional institutional copy and activity modules. Official business activity, products/services, proof, location, and contact channels remain to be supplied and validated.
